## September 11 revision: shared-library governance and uncertainty

Read the full HTML of [Paglieri et al., arXiv:2609.04170v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04170v1), especially sections 2.1, 3.5–3.6 and 4. Shared-library invalidity and an unmonitored complaint endpoint motivate the new experiment. Effective sanctions and collective rule changes are proposals in that paper, not established treatment effects. Our 288-cell deterministic adaptation scripts both reuse and reporting; it does not reproduce emergent discovery or whistleblowing. See `docs/commons-response-design.md`.

The [NIST Wilson interval reference](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm) supports the binary uncertainty correction. The correction is post-run analysis; raw model choices and frozen study inputs are unchanged. See `docs/analysis-revision.md`.
