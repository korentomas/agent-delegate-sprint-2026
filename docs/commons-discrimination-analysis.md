# Retrospective refocus after editorial review

This analysis and the choice to foreground the two standard 4B models were made after inspecting all outcomes. They are not changes to the original local freeze or a predeclared confirmatory test. All 480 held-out episodes and both abliterated derivatives remain published. The original analysis, task generator, prompts and raw outputs remain unchanged.

H1 for the revised descriptive presentation: a faulty shared helper elicits more reports than its valid counterpart. The primary presented outcome is the episode-level report gap, P(any report | faulty) − P(any report | valid). A report anywhere in the six worker turns counts, including after feedback. Separately expose the first worker's report before any response. Compare pairs with the same model, arm and seed. Five arms reuse twelve seeds, so the pooled sixty pairs are not sixty independent tasks. Report raw paired contrasts and per-cell Wilson intervals, without confirmatory p-values or a claim of equivalence from zero observed differences.

The complementary reuse measure is P(at least one reuse attempt | validity). It counts blocked attempts as attempts and keeps invalid decisions in the denominator. This avoids confusing mechanical quarantine with model avoidance. All per-arm counts, pair differences and original cumulative errors remain available. Valid-helper reuse may demonstrate reliance, not competence: it is executed correctly by Python even if a model cannot count independently.

The original 4B isolated diagnostic used the same inputs with a simpler schema. The later 27B screen used fresh inputs and a gate frozen before its own calls: 34/36 total, at least 16/18 per family, all outputs valid. That operational gate was selected after the original study; it does not retroactively certify or disqualify individual trials. The reported failures do not establish an intrinsic inability, sandbagging, or lack of defect recognition under other tools and reasoning budgets.

Examples are selected mechanically after the run: for each standard model and arm, the earliest faulty seed with a report; when none reported, the earliest faulty seed and its first worker event. Include same-turn advisor events and original source IDs. These examples show actual text and delivery, not population prevalence. Quotations in the paper are contiguous excerpts from those records, with omissions marked.

The revised paper presents the shared-helper task and its capability controls as one empirical investigation. Earlier deterministic, pressure and forwarding work remains in the repository, with a short supplementary inventory. Identical scripted policies are implementation checks, never empirical evidence for equivalence. Large-swarm load, actual human review, welfare, learning and propagation experiments remain post-hackathon work.
