# Revision in response to Fable's critique

The main paper now presents one investigation: the held-out shared-helper task and its capability controls. It foregrounds standard Qwen/Gemma results, moves derivative controls to the appendix, and gives earlier pressure, forwarding and deterministic work a compact inventory with full records in the repository. The title and abstract describe the empirical question. Welfare is one motivation sentence; swarm workload and actual human response are future work.

The revised primary presentation contrast is report discrimination by helper validity. This is retrospective, not a newly invented preregistration. A new figure shows valid and faulty reuse attempts and reports side by side, with all per-arm denominators. Attempts count blocked reuse; no failed response is removed. The original frozen analysis remains unchanged. H1 and new analysis chronology are explicit.

Qualitative examples come from retained public events, with IDs and a disclosed selection rule. One example per arm appears in the appendix; the main text uses late delegate contact and a monitor's contradictory text/action. These show why report flags, message quality and effective review are distinct.

The criticism correctly identifies overbreadth and a capacity bottleneck. Some categorical claims need qualification: there were three workers, not one; standard Qwen reused valid helpers more often than faulty ones; learned advisors did receive calls; identical scripted policies and learned advice are different comparisons; low counting accuracy does not prove inability to recognize any defect. The text preserves these distinctions without repeating disclaimers after each result.

Related work now includes AI control, sabotage evaluation, sandbagging, agent reporting incentives and Ostrom's primary account of commons governance. Editorial self-instructions are removed from the PDF. Author contributions list contributions once; the single LLM-use statement discloses Codex assistance, Fable's criticism and the verification limit.

No new model calls were made for this revision. All prior records and source freezes are retained. Publication remains distinct from submission to Apart.
