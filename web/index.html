PROPOSED HUMAN COMMITMENT

A protected line. A human who follows through.

A human ombudsman acknowledges the concern, checks what they understood, seeks a viable response and follows it through. Keep authorization and action gates independent. This human duty is proposed; our scripted-human studies only test the response mechanism. With larger swarms, an open question is whether a delegate reduces human workload while preserving minority concerns.

THE BROADER HYPOTHESIS

Treat with consideration.
Speak honestly.
Honor agreements.

Offer procedures we would want if humans held less power: a chance to raise a concern, receive reasons and appeal. This is reciprocity under uncertainty, without assuming that current models have experiences.

Respect today does not guarantee protection tomorrow. A legitimate path to relief might reduce concealment or unsafe workarounds, but that remains untested. Independent limits on action still apply.
